Everything for your day and your wallet

Plan your work on a board, see which bills are still unpaid, and watch your savings grow, all in one app.

Drag-and-drop task board

Open, In Progress, On Hold and Done. Drag cards between columns and set a priority on each one.

Rich notes and projects

Write task details with bullets, numbering, checklists and pasted images, and group tasks by project.

Bills that reset every month

Red when unpaid, amber when due soon, green when paid. Every bill starts fresh on the 1st.

Savings goals

Set a target for each goal, add money whenever you like, and see how much you need each month to get there.

Salary and overtime

Log your OT hours and LifeSync works out the pay at normal, rest-day and public-holiday rates.

Syncs live, works offline

Changes show up on your other device in seconds. No signal? Keep working, and it syncs when you're back online.

Reminders

Get a notification before a task is due or a bill needs paying, plus an optional morning summary.

One dashboard

Today's tasks, unpaid bills, savings progress and the money left this month, all on one screen.

Private to you

Sign in with your own account. Your tasks and finances can only be seen by you.
